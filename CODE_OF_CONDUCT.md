# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are dedicated to fostering a welcoming and respectful environment for all participants in our community. We value diversity and believe that every member deserves to engage without fear of discrimination or harassment, regardless of their background, identity, or characteristics.

We commit to creating a space where collaboration, learning, and mutual respect are the foundation of all interactions.

## Expected Behavior

To maintain a positive and productive community, we encourage all members to:

- **Show respect and consideration** for others' perspectives, backgrounds, and contributions
- **Communicate thoughtfully** and assume good intentions in discussions
- **Provide helpful feedback** in a constructive and supportive manner
- **Take ownership** of mistakes, offer sincere apologies when needed, and use errors as learning opportunities
- **Prioritize collective success** by considering the community's wellbeing alongside individual goals
- **Welcome newcomers** and help them feel included and valued
- **Listen actively** and create space for diverse voices to be heard

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated in our community:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory conduct of any kind
- Inappropriate or unwelcome sexual language, imagery, or advances
- Disruptive behavior including trolling, inflammatory comments, or deliberate provocation
- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning remarks
- Sharing others' personal or private information without explicit consent
- Sustained disruption of discussions, events, or community activities
- Any conduct that creates an unsafe, hostile, or exclusionary environment

## Responsibilities of Community Leaders

Community leaders and moderators are tasked with upholding these standards. Their responsibilities include:

- Interpreting and applying this Code of Conduct fairly and consistently
- Addressing violations through appropriate and proportionate responses
- Removing or moderating content that violates community standards
- Providing clear explanations for moderation actions when warranted
- Protecting the confidentiality of those who report concerns

## Where This Applies

This Code of Conduct governs behavior in all official community spaces, including:

- Online platforms (forums, chat channels, social media accounts, repositories)
- In-person and virtual events
- Any situation where someone is representing the community publicly

## Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it to the community leadership team at **thriveengineer@gmail.com**. 

All reports will be:
- Reviewed promptly and thoroughly
- Handled with discretion and confidentiality
- Investigated fairly with appropriate follow-up action

## Enforcement Process

When violations occur, community leaders will apply consequences based on the severity and context of the situation:

### Level 1: Guidance
**Situation:** Minor infractions such as inappropriate language or unprofessional conduct.

**Response:** Private communication from moderators clarifying the issue and explaining why the behavior was problematic. The individual may be asked to apologize publicly.

### Level 2: Formal Warning
**Situation:** A notable violation or pattern of concerning behavior.

**Response:** Official warning with clear expectations for future conduct. The individual must avoid contact with affected parties and may be restricted from certain community spaces for a defined period. Further violations may result in escalated consequences.

### Level 3: Temporary Suspension
**Situation:** Significant breach of community standards or ongoing inappropriate behavior.

**Response:** Temporary removal from all community participation for a specified timeframe. No interaction with community members or leaders is permitted during this period. Violation of suspension terms will result in permanent removal.

### Level 4: Permanent Removal
**Situation:** Severe or repeated violations, including harassment, threats, or persistent disregard for community standards.

**Response:** Permanent ban from all community spaces and activities.

## Acknowledgments

This Code of Conduct draws inspiration from widely-adopted community guidelines and represents our shared values and expectations for respectful participation.
